Google Workspace Business Starter is a bundle: Gmail on your domain, storage, meetings, and the rest of the starter suite. Mailstead is a webmail application you run, with Resend carrying mail in and out. If you need the bundle, Starter is the product. If you need mailboxes and already operate a small app, the bundle is a different purchase.

Prices below are Google’s US dollar list, checked on 14 October 2026. The [general cost guide](/guides/custom-domain-email-cost/) explains domains and admin time. This page stays on Workspace.

## List price, not the introduction

| Plan | Commitment | Price | Storage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Business Starter, Flexible | None. Billed monthly for the accounts you have | $8.40 per user per month | 30 GB pooled per user |
| Business Starter, annual | One year for the licences you buy at the start | $7 per user per month, or $84 per user per year | 30 GB pooled per user |

Source: [Compare Flexible and annual plans](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/billing/compare-flexible-and-annual-fixed-term-payment-plans) and [business editions](https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/getting-started/editions/business-editions), checked 2026-10-14. Business Starter, Standard, and Plus stop at 300 users.

[workspace.google.com/pricing](https://workspace.google.com/pricing) also showed an introductory price for new customers, limited to the first 20 users and 12 months, with standard pricing afterward. Do not put that introduction in a renewal forecast. Canadian list prices on the same help page are C$11.00 (Flexible) and C$9.20 (annual) per user per month. They are Google’s CAD prices, not a conversion, and they are not subtracted from the USD totals elsewhere on this site.

## What the money buys

| | Mailstead | Business Starter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inbox | Browser app you host: folders, compose, reply, forward, attachments, search | Gmail |
| Licence | $0 software. Hosting and Resend are separate | Per user, as in the table |
| Docs, Drive, Meet | Not included | Included in the suite |
| Desktop mail protocol | Not provided | Not compared here; this page does not restate client setup |
| Spam handling | Local rules and DMARC-fail routing to Junk. No efficacy percentage | Google markets phishing and spam protection on the pricing page. This site does not repeat an unverified block rate |
| Who operates it | You deploy, back up, and watch quotas | Google operates Gmail |

An illustrative Mailstead month is on the [pricing page](/pricing/#scenario): 20 messages per mailbox, Hobby hosting, Resend chosen from that volume. For 10 mailboxes that illustration stays on Resend Free and a $5 Hobby minimum if usage is inside the included credit. Ten Starter users on the annual list are $70 per month before tax. The $65 gap is not “savings,” because Starter includes the suite and a vendor-operated mailbox, and the illustration collapses as soon as message volume leaves Resend Free.

## Who should pick which

Choose Workspace if several people expect Drive, Docs, Meet, or an admin console Google already runs. Choose Mailstead if the requirement is custom-domain webmail, the team can run one container, and the missing suite is acceptable. A company that wants both mail and the office apps should buy Workspace and ignore the mailbox-only arithmetic.

Mailstead does not include end-to-end encryption, a compliance vault, or a promise that the 10–50 person design target has been load-tested.
